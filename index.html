04 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
hit the road.

What freight services can I request?+

Request full truckload, less-than-truckload, or dedicated transportation. Include the commodity, weight, dimensions, and equipment requirements so the shipment can be reviewed.

What information do I need for a quote?+

Start with pickup and delivery ZIP codes, the freight type, total weight, and your preferred pickup date. Dimensions, pallet count, and any special handling requirements help produce an accurate quote.

Can I request a recurring lane?+

Yes. Choose Dedicated freight in the quote form and describe the route, shipping frequency, and preferred schedule in the shipment notes.

How do I confirm a shipment?+

A quote request starts the conversation. Equipment, pricing, and the pickup schedule must be confirmed before booking. This design preview prepares a downloadable request; it does not book or send a shipment.
